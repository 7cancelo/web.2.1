Pastoral couple

Pastor Dr. Brett & Marisa Smitsdorff

Brett and Marisa joined the staff of Trinity on 1 February 2024. They have been in ministry together since 1995, beginning as youth leaders and pastors at Milnerton Baptist Church.

They later served as the pastoral couple at Mthatha Baptist Church and at Greenfield Randfontein Baptist Church, where they served for 15 years.

A lifetime of ministry.

Teaching · Shepherding · Equipping

Brett was studying at Cape Town Baptist Seminary when he and Marisa were called to begin a youth ministry at Milnerton Baptist Church. Through street evangelism the ministry grew and became a formative part of their pastoral calling.

Marisa is presently studying through SATS and BTC and is passionate about Children’s Ministry. Brett and Marisa have three boys; their youngest completed high school at the end of 2022.

  • Licentiate in Theology (Cum Laude) · Cape Town Baptist Seminary · 1996
  • Master’s Degree in Theology · Cape Town Baptist Seminary and University of Pretoria · 2004
  • PhD in Practical Theology · Cape Town Baptist Seminary and University of Pretoria · 2012
