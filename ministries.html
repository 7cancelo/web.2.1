Life together

A place for every person in every season.

Find a community to grow with, a team to serve alongside and a meaningful way to be part of church life.

Connect · Grow · Serve

We offer a range of ministries for the whole family.

Most programmes meet weekly, while event-based groups are announced through the weekly bulletin. Please contact the church office if you would like to get involved.

Family Ministry

01 / Together

Trinity Kidz

Sunday · 09:30

Children’s Church runs in Trinity Hall during the Sunday service for ages 2–13, with Bible stories, practical life lessons, memory verses, crafts, activities, singing and dancing. Contact Marisa · 084 205 5892.

Lite4Life

Friday · 18:00

A youth programme for high school students with practical Bible teaching, discussion, worship and fellowship.

Trinity YA

Friday · 18:00

Young adults aged 18–35 gather for fellowship over tea, worship, prayer and the Word. Contact Afikile · 083 928 5676.

Trinity Ladies

Events only

Ladies meet every second month for worship, fellowship and encouragement through God’s Word. Contact Jacqui · 084 510 4810.

Forge (Men)

Events only

A space for men to gather for fellowship, encouragement and open discussion around God’s Word—over breakfast, a hike, sport or prayer.

Retired Persons Fellowship

Events only

Relaxed gatherings during the year for fellowship, worship, encouragement, tea and good conversation.
